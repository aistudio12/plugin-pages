# Support

Help for Faceless Reels Video Maker and Animation Maker AI.

Contact [rhua4070@gmail.com](mailto:rhua4070@gmail.com) with a question, reproducible problem or feature suggestion. Include the plugin name in the subject. There is no guaranteed response time or around-the-clock support service.

## Report a problem

Tell us which plugin you used, what you wanted to accomplish, what happened, and what you expected. Include the version if available. A short, redacted example is usually more useful than an entire conversation.

Only send material you are comfortable sharing for support. Remove passwords, API keys, private client details, payment information and other sensitive content from screenshots and examples.

## The plugin does not seem to activate

Check that it is installed and available in your ChatGPT account. Start a new conversation and name the plugin and task explicitly. For example, ask Animation Maker AI for a timed storyboard, or ask Faceless Reels Video Maker to improve a video's opening.

Availability can depend on ChatGPT account, platform and product settings. We cannot change OpenAI account access or subscription entitlements.

## I received a plan rather than a finished video

These packages supply skills rather than a rendering backend. A script, scene table or generation prompt is a production asset, not an exported video. Use a suitable tool available in your session or take the handoff to your preferred editor or generator.

## How do I get better results?

Provide the audience, destination, approximate runtime and production style when you know them. Share an existing script or reference you have permission to use. For revisions, identify the scene or passage to change and what should stay consistent.

## Recommended tools, payments and referrals

You choose whether to use any external service. Its provider handles accounts, credits, subscriptions, cancellations and billing. Contact that provider for those issues; we cannot access or change third-party accounts.

Affiliate links are disabled in the initial packages. If enabled in a future release, the recommendation will disclose the potential commission. A recommendation is optional and does not guarantee a particular outcome.

## Accuracy, rights or privacy concerns

Email the plugin name and a minimal redacted example. Identify what needs correction. For requests relating to support correspondence, explain what information you want us to locate, correct or delete. Do not send identity documents unless a necessary verification process has been agreed.
