# Creative Workflow Plugins

AI animation maker and faceless video maker workflows for animated stories, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok videos.

## Animation Maker AI

34 focused ChatGPT skills for animation scripts, original character design, consistent visual styles, storyboards, timing sheets and shot-by-shot AI animation prompts. Includes workflows for Blender, 2D animation, motion graphics, animated explainers, stop motion, voiceover, lip-sync planning and web animation code.

Try: Plan a 30-second animated story about a small robot returning a lost plant. Include character anchors, six shots, timing and sound direction.

## Faceless Reels Video Maker

36 focused ChatGPT skills for faceless YouTube videos, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok. Develop video ideas, hooks, narration, timed scene plans, AI visual prompts, b-roll keywords, captions, titles and thumbnail concepts.

Try: Plan a 45-second vertical explainer about why cats purr, with narration, visuals and three opening ideas.
